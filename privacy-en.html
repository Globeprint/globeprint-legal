Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Globeprint is a travel-diary app made by Zeiler Odair Cubillos Rincon, an individual based in Bogotá, Colombia, who is the data controller. Contact: globeprint.soporte@gmail.com.

In short: almost everything you keep in Globeprint (diary, photos, trips, countries, steps) lives only on your phone. Only what you choose to share, or what a feature you turn on needs, reaches our servers. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across apps.

1. Data that stays on your phone

  • Diary, album photos, trips, route notes, visited countries and places, trip preparation, passports and reminders.
  • Your location to draw your steps and verify places (also in the background if you turn on “My steps”).
  • Location of your gallery photos to place them on the map; face detection to blur other people, done on the device.
  • Address-book contacts: read only to suggest travel contacts; your address book is not uploaded.

2. Data sent to our servers (only if you use that feature)

  • Account: identifier, name, email and profile photo from Google, Facebook or Apple if you sign in (or a guest session).
  • Traveler profile (if you create it): @username, name, photo, bio, country; visited countries by level, album progress, photos you mark public (other people's faces blurred), public highlights and routes you publish. With a private account only people you accept can see them.
  • Follow: who you follow and who follows you; blocks and reports.
  • Group trips: name, photo, role and your live location only during the trip, visible to group members.
  • Safe Travel (if enabled): recent location and 48-hour track, medical and document notes you write, lodging, travel contacts and family emails, to alert them in an emergency.
  • Shared routes: the route data you decide to share (places, prices, tips), without photos or personal data.
  • Notifications: the device push token.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe to Plus, Apple or Google process the payment; we only receive the subscription status (via RevenueCat).

3. Why we use it

Only to provide the features you use: store your profile, show your content to whom you allow, coordinate group trips, alert your family in emergencies, send notifications and manage your subscription. We don't use your data for advertising, sell it, or use it to train AI.

4. Who it is shared with (processors)

  • Supabase (database, authentication and cloud storage).
  • Expo (push notifications), Resend (emergency emails), RevenueCat, Apple and Google (subscriptions).
  • Services the app calls directly from your phone to display information (they receive your IP address and the query or coordinates): Open-Meteo (weather), Photon/OpenStreetMap (search), OpenFreeMap (street map), NASA (Earth imagery), Wikimedia Commons (sample photos), USGS and GDACS (alerts, from our server).
  • Authorities, only when required by law.

Partner (affiliate) links. Some buttons for hotels, tours, transfers, car rentals, travel insurance and eSIMs open partner websites (Booking.com, GetYourGuide, Viator, Discover Cars, SafetyWing, Airalo). These links carry a Globeprint code so the partner knows you came from the app and pays us a small commission if you book, at no extra cost to you. Globeprint does not send them your name, email, location or photos; what you do on their site is governed by each partner’s privacy policy.

5. Retention

While you keep your account. The Safe Travel track is deleted after 48 hours. Public photos you delete or make private are removed from the server. If you delete your account, everything on the server is deleted immediately (technical backups are overwritten within 30 days).

6. Your rights

You can access, update, correct and delete your data and withdraw consent (Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and equivalent laws in your country, such as the GDPR). In the app: Profile › edit your profile, change privacy, and Profile › Delete my account. Or email globeprint.soporte@gmail.com. We reply within 15 business days.

7. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), per-user access rules in the database, private photo storage with temporary links, and a Face ID/fingerprint vault for hidden content on your phone.

8. Children

Globeprint is not directed to children under 13. Minors should use the app with their parents' or guardians' permission.

9. Changes

We'll let you know in the app if this policy changes.

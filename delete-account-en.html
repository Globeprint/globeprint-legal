Delete your Globeprint account

In the app (immediate): open Globeprint › Profile tab › at the bottom, Delete my account › confirm.

Without the app: email globeprint.soporte@gmail.com from your account's email with the subject “Delete my account” and your @username. We delete it within 15 business days and confirm.

What is deleted

Everything on our servers: account, traveler profile, followers and following, public photos, highlights, shared routes, group trips, Safe Travel file, locations and emergency contacts, reports you made and the push token.

What is kept

Nothing on the server; technical backups are overwritten within 30 days. Your diary, photos and trips stored on your phone are not touched: uninstall the app to remove them. If you subscribe to Plus, cancel it in your Apple or Google account settings.
